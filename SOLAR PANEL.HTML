Series and parallel comparison

Theory, equations and model assumptions

Solar input power

Psolar = solar irradiance × total panel area

Photovoltaic efficiency

ηPV = Pelectrical / Psolar
Pelectrical = ηPVPsolar

Electrical power

P = VI

Each representative panel operates at approximately 36.4 V at its maximum-power point when sufficient sunlight is available.

Ipanel = Ppanel / Vpanel

Identical panels in series

Vseries = V₁ + V₂
Iseries = Ipanel

Series connection gives a higher voltage without adding the panel currents.

Identical panels in parallel

Vparallel = Vpanel
Iparallel = I₁ + I₂

Parallel connection gives a higher current without adding the panel voltages.

Checking electrical power

Parray = VarrayIarray

For two identical panels under equal illumination, this gives approximately the same total power for series and parallel connections.

Controller and pump efficiencies

Ppump input = ηcontrollerPPV
Pwater = ηpumpPpump input

Water lifting

Ep = mgh
Pwater = ρgQh
Q = Pwater / (ρgh)

Student investigation

  1. Set solar radiation to 800 W/m² and total panel area to 2.0 m².
  2. Select series and record voltage, current and power.
  3. Select parallel and record voltage, current and power.
  4. Explain why the voltage and current change but total power does not.
  5. Compare flow rate at pumping heads of 10 m, 60 m and 100 m.
  6. Reduce solar radiation until the pump stops. Record the start threshold.
